About This Course

MATH 1130 — College Algebra (3 Credit Hours)

Course Overview

This course provides a solid foundation in college algebra. You will study linear, quadratic, polynomial, rational, radical, exponential, and logarithmic functions, with emphasis on applications of these concepts to real-world problems. By the end of the course, you will be able to connect symbolic algebra with graphical and contextual representations.
