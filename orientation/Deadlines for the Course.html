Unit 1 Deadline

Homework and quizzes for Unit 1 are available until September 22 in MyLab Math Student Links.

Test 1

We will take Test 1 during the regular class period on Wednesday, Sept 25. Review materials are in the MyLab Math content pages. Coverage includes: polynomials, factoring, function definitions/graphs, and graphs of polynomial functions (i.e., all Unit 1 topics).

Unit 2 Deadline

Homework and quizzes for Unit 2 are available until October 27 in MyLab Math Student Links.

Test 2

We will take Test 2 during the regular class period on Wednesday, Oct 30. Review materials are in the MyLab Math content pages. Coverage includes: rational expressions, vertical and horizontal asymptotes, and radical expressions/equations (i.e., all Unit 2 topics).

Unit 3 Deadline

Homework and quizzes for Unit 3 are available until December 8 in MyLab Math Student Links.

Final Exam

The Final Exam is on Monday, Dec 9. The duration is approximately 2 hours. It is cumulative, with an emphasis on Unit 3 (~60%) and material from Units 1–2 (~40%). Be sure to complete the Final Exam review in the content pages.

These deadlines also appear on the Online Campus calendar. Always verify the dates shown in MyLab Math.
