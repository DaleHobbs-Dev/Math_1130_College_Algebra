About Me

I grew up in Lawrenceburg, TN and got my start at Columbia State Community College before transferring to Vanderbilt University. After finishing my bachelor’s degree, I began adjunct teaching at Columbia State and discovered that I genuinely love the community college classroom—especially helping students build confidence with math.

To deepen my preparation, I earned a Master’s in Mathematics from Tennessee Tech University and have taught full‑time at Columbia State since Fall 2012. The best part of the job is working directly with students—breaking big ideas into manageable steps, celebrating small wins, and showing that with practice and support, you can do mathematics.
