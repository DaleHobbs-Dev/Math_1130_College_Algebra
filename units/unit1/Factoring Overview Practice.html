Worksheet: Factoring Practice Overview

Directions: Show your work. Factor completely. (When solving equations, check your solutions.)

1) Factor Out the Greatest Common Factor (GCF)

  1. $12x + 18$

  2. $15a^2b - 25ab^2$

  3. $8x^3y + 12x^2y^2 - 4xy$

  4. $-18m^4n^2 + 30m^3n^5 - 6m^2n$

2) Factoring by Grouping

  1. $3x(x+4) + 5(x+4)$

  2. $xy + 6y + 5x + 30$

  3. $4a^2 + 2ab - 8a - 4b$

3) Factor Trinomials of the Form $x^2+bx+c$

  1. $x^2 + 7x + 10$

  2. $x^2 - 11x + 24$

  3. $x^2 + 3x - 40$

  4. $3x^2 - 15x - 72$

4) Factor Trinomials of the Form $ax^2+bx+c$ (where $a \ne 1$)

  1. $2x^2 + 7x + 3$

  2. $3x^2 - 11x - 4$

  3. $6x^2 + x - 15$

5) Special Factoring

Difference of Squares: $a^2 - b^2 = (a-b)(a+b)$

Difference of Cubes: $a^3 - b^3 = (a-b)(a^2 + ab + b^2)$

Sum of Cubes: $a^3 + b^3 = (a+b)(a^2 - ab + b^2)$

All of these factoring situations involve expressions with Blank terms.

  1. $x^2 - 64$

  2. $9y^2 - 25$

  3. $x^3 - 27$

  4. $8m^3 + 125$

6) Solving Equations Using Factoring

Zero-Product Theorem: If a product of factors equals zero, then at least one of the factors has to be Blank.

  1. $(x-4)(x+9)=0$

  2. $x^2 - 5x - 24 = 0$

  3. $2x^2 + 7x = 15 $

  4. $3(x^2 + 3) = 12x$
