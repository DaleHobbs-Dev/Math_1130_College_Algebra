Factoring Trinomials

Module Learning Outcomes

  • Factor trinomials of the form $x^2+bx+c$ using the Sum and Product (Guess and Check) method.
  • Factor trinomials of the form $ax^2+bx+c$ using Factoring by Grouping.
  • Identify and factor out the GCF first (when present) to simplify factoring.

Thinking Backwards: Multiplication to Factoring

We saw that $(x-2)(x+5)$ multiplies into $x^2+3x-10$. In this section, we want to think backwards and develop the process for factoring $x^2+3x-10$ back into Blank.

Note: Always factor out the Blank first (if there is one)! It will simplify the factoring in the long-run.

Example 1.

Factor the following by using the Sum and Product method or the Guess and Check method.

  1. $x^2+8x+15$
  2. $x^2-10x+24$
  3. $x^2+7x-30$
  4. $6x^3+24x^2-30x$
  5. $3y^2+6y+6$

When the Leading Coefficient Isn’t 1

These past examples were all polynomials of the form $x^2+bx+c$ where the squared term had a $1$ in its spot. The next examples are a step above this in the form of $ax^2+bx+c$ and take a bit more work to factor.

Example 2.

Factor the following by using the Factoring by Grouping method or the Guess and Check method.

  1. $3x^2+13x+4$
  2. $2x^2+13x+6$
  3. $5x^2+13x-6$
  4. $24x^2y^2-42xy^2+9y^2$
  5. $4x^2+28xy+49y^2$
