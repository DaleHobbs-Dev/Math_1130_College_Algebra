Multiplying Polynomials

Key Properties for Multiplying

Commutative Property of Multiplication: $a\cdot b = b \cdot a$

Example: $(7y^2)(4y^5) = (4y^5)(7y^2)$

Distributive Property of Multiplication: $a(b+c)=a \cdot b+a \cdot c$

Example: $3(x+4)=3\cdot x + 3\cdot 4 = 3x+12$
