AC Method Factoring Practice

Directions: Factor completely using the AC method. Show your work.

Using the AC-Method Organizer

First factor out any greatest common factor. For a trinomial of the form $ax^2+bx+c$, write the product $ac$ at the top of the X and the sum $b$ at the bottom. Write two numbers on the left and right that multiply to $ac$ and add to $b$. Use those numbers to split the middle term, then factor by grouping.

Practice

  1. $8x^2+26x+6$

  2. $4y^2+16y-9$

  3. $5v^2-18v+16$
