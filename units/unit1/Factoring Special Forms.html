Factoring Special Products

Difference of Squares

Difference of Squares: $a^2 - b^2 = (a-b)(a+b)$

Tip: Always factor out a GCF first (if there is one), then check if what remains is a difference of squares.

Sum and Difference of Cubes

Difference of Cubes: $a^3 - b^3 = (a-b)(a^2 + ab + b^2)$

Sum of Cubes: $a^3 + b^3 = (a+b)(a^2 - ab + b^2)$

Tip: Look for perfect cubes first. If there’s a GCF, factor it out before using these patterns.
