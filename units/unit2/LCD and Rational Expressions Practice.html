LCD and Rational Expressions Practice

Directions: Show your work. Factor denominators completely when needed. Leave each LCD in factored form. For addition and subtraction, simplify your final answer and state all values excluded by the original denominators.

Unlike Denominators

To find an LCD, use the least common multiple of the numerical coefficients and each distinct variable factor raised to the highest power appearing in either denominator. To add or subtract, multiply each numerator and denominator by the missing factors, combine the numerators, and simplify. When subtracting, subtract the entire second numerator.

Example 1.

Find the LCD for the two rational expressions given in each part.

  1. $\cfrac{3}{x}$ and $\cfrac{2}{x+4}$

  2. $\cfrac{5}{6x^2}$ and $\cfrac{1}{4x}$

  3. $\cfrac{x+1}{x-3}$ and $\cfrac{2x-5}{x+3}$

  4. $\cfrac{2}{x^2-16}$ and $\cfrac{3}{x+4}$

  1. $\cfrac{1}{x^2+5x+6}$ and $\cfrac{2}{x^2+x-6}$

  2. $\cfrac{x}{(x-1)^2}$ and $\cfrac{3}{x(x-1)}$

  3. $\cfrac{2}{2x^2+7x+3}$ and $\cfrac{1}{2x^2-x-1}$

  4. $\cfrac{x}{x-2}$ and $\cfrac{4}{x^2-4}$

Example 2.

Use the LCD information from example 1 in order to carry out the addition or subtraction.

Each letter uses the same two rational expressions as the corresponding letter in Example 1. Rewrite both expressions with the LCD, combine, and simplify.

  1. $\cfrac{3}{x}+\cfrac{2}{x+4}$

  2. $\cfrac{5}{6x^2}-\cfrac{1}{4x}$

  3. $\cfrac{x+1}{x-3}-\cfrac{2x-5}{x+3}$

  4. $\cfrac{2}{x^2-16}+\cfrac{3}{x+4}$

  1. $\cfrac{1}{x^2+5x+6}+\cfrac{2}{x^2+x-6}$

  2. $\cfrac{x}{(x-1)^2}-\cfrac{3}{x(x-1)}$

  3. $\cfrac{2}{2x^2+7x+3}+\cfrac{1}{2x^2-x-1}$

  4. $\cfrac{x}{x-2}-\cfrac{4}{x^2-4}$
