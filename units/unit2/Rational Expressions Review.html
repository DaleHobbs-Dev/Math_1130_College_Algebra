Additional Practice: Rational Expressions

1. Domain of a Rational Function

Give the domain of each rational function. Use either set-builder notation or interval notation.

  1. $f(x)=\cfrac{2x+5}{(x-3)(x+4)}$

  1. $g(x)=\cfrac{x-1}{x^2+x-6}$

2. Simplifying Rational Expressions

Simplify each rational expression completely.

  1. $\cfrac{6x}{3x}$

  2. $\cfrac{x^2-9}{x+3}$

  1. $\cfrac{x^2+5x+6}{x+2}$

  2. $\cfrac{x^2-4}{x^2+5x+6}$

3. Multiplying and Dividing Rational Expressions

Multiply or divide as indicated. Simplify your final answer completely.

  1. $\cfrac{x^2-1}{x^2+5x+6}\cdot\cfrac{x+3}{x-1}$

  1. $\cfrac{x^2-9}{x}\div\cfrac{x+3}{2x}$

4. Adding and Subtracting Rational Expressions with the Same Denominator

Add or subtract as indicated. Simplify when possible.

  1. $\cfrac{3x}{x+1}+\cfrac{5}{x+1}$

  2. $\cfrac{x+4}{x-2}+\cfrac{2x-1}{x-2}$

  1. $\cfrac{7x-3}{x+5}-\cfrac{2x+1}{x+5}$

  2. $\cfrac{x^2-9}{x^2-4}-\cfrac{x+2}{x^2-4}$

5. Finding the Least Common Denominator

Find the least common denominator (LCD) for each set of denominators.

  1. $x$ and $x+4$

  2. $x-3$ and $x^2-9$

  1. $x^2$ , $x$, and $x^4$

  2. $x+2$ and $x^2+5x+6$

6. Building Equivalent Rational Expressions

Fill in the missing numerator so the two rational expressions are equivalent. Do not simplify your answer.

  1. $\cfrac{3}{x}=\cfrac{\,?\!}{x(x+2)}$

  2. $\cfrac{x+1}{x-4}=\cfrac{\,?\!}{(x-4)(x+3)}$

  1. $\cfrac{2x}{x+5}=\cfrac{\,?\!}{y^2(x+5)}$

  2. $\cfrac{x-2}{x^2-9}=\cfrac{\,?\!}{(x-3)(x+3)(x+1)}$

7. Solving Equations Involving Rational Expressions

Identify the LCD then use it to solve the equation. Be sure to check for any extraneous solutions.

  1. $\cfrac{x}{3}+\cfrac{2}{3}=\cfrac{5}{3}$

  2. $\cfrac{4}{x}=\cfrac{2}{x-3}$

  3. $\cfrac{1}{x}+\cfrac{1}{x+2}=\cfrac{3}{x+2}$
